# Microfluidic Foundry Services — Detailed Comparison

> Research date: March 2026

## Overview

This document provides a comprehensive comparison of microfluidic foundry services and contract manufacturers worldwide, covering capabilities, materials, pricing (where available), minimum order quantities, lead times, certifications, and specialties.

---

## 1. Microfluidic ChipShop (Jena, Germany)

**Website:** https://www.microfluidic-chipshop.com/

**Founded:** 2002 (spin-off of Fraunhofer Institute for Applied Optics and Precision Engineering)

**Specialty:** World market leader in lab-on-a-chip systems; full value chain from design through to production.

### Capabilities
- Complete technology chain: microstructure design, mold-insert fabrication, polymer replication (injection molding, hot embossing, casting), mechanical processing, biochemical surface functionalization, reagent storage, and industrial QC
- In-house toolmaking department for injection molding
- Direct precision micromilling for rapid prototyping (single-piece capability)
- All molding machines housed in ISO Class 7 cleanrooms

### Materials
- High-performance thermoplastics: COC, COP, PC, PS, PMMA, PP
- Soft elastomeric materials: TPE
- Also works with ceramics, silicon, glass, metal, and hybrid systems

### Products
- Capillary electrophoresis chips, micromixers, nanoliter plates, fluidic interfaces, fluidic platforms, polymer substrates

### Certifications
- DIN EN ISO 9001
- DIN ISO 13485

### MOQ / Lead Times / Pricing
- Prototyping from single piece (via micromilling)
- Injection molding MOQ: not publicly listed; contact required
- Pricing: not publicly listed

## 2. Micronit (Enschede, Netherlands)

**Website:** https://micronit.com/ | **Store:** https://store.micronit.com/

- 600 m2 wafer-based microfabrication cleanroom (ISO Class 5-8)

- 200 mm wafer processing

### Materials

- Silicon

- Hybrid combinations

- Off-the-shelf chips available via online store

### Certifications
- ISO 13485:2016
- ISO 9001:2015

### MOQ / Lead Times / Pricing
- Catalog chips available individually via online store
- Custom development: contact required for pricing

## 4. Dolomite Microfluidics (Royston, UK)

**Website:** https://www.dolomite-microfluidics.com/ (now part of Unchained Labs)

**Specialty:** Modular microfluidic systems; droplet generation at scale.

### Capabilities
- Design and manufacture of standard and custom microfluidic components
- Complete modular systems: pumps, chips, connectors, temperature controllers, sensors, software
- Custom chip design with expert consultation on junction geometry
- Telos high-throughput system: up to 70 parallel junctions, ~1 tonne of monodisperse droplets/month

### Products
- Telos 2 reagent chips (30 and 50 um versions)
- Droplet generators for 5-75 um droplets
- Encapsulation chips for cells, beads, particles
- Full range of microfluidic accessories

### MOQ / Lead Times / Pricing
- Standard chips available for purchase
- Custom design service: contact required

## 6. STRATEC (Birkenfeld, Germany / Salzburg, Austria)

**Website:** https://www.stratec.com/consumables

**Specialty:** IVD consumables and fully automated analyzer systems; microfluidic manufacturing derived from CD/DVD disc manufacturing.

### Capabilities
- Working with microfluidics since 2009
- Lithographic processes for microfluidic structures
- Variothermal and compression injection molding
- Aspect ratios up to 12:1; structure sizes down to 150 nm
- Thermal compression bonding, solvent-supported low-temperature polymer bonding, laser welding
- Alignment tolerances: +/- 5 um; z-height precision: +/- 20 nm
- Coating of injection molded parts
- Packaging and labeling
- In-house automation capabilities

### Materials
- Injection molded polymers with various surface coatings

### Certifications
- ISO 13485 (Salzburg facility)

### MOQ / Lead Times / Pricing
- OEM-focused; typically serves large diagnostic instrument companies
- Not a prototyping service; oriented toward high-volume production

## 13. Toppan (Tokyo, Japan)

**Website:** https://www.holdings.toppan.com/

**Specialty:** Mass production of glass microfluidic chips using LCD color filter manufacturing technology.

### Capabilities
- Photolithography-based glass microfluidic chip fabrication
- Adapted from LCD color filter manufacturing (large glass substrate processing)
- Channel width: 10 um to several mm
- Channel depth: 1 to 50 um
- Multi-imposition on large glass substrates for cost reduction
- Properties equivalent or superior to PDMS chips

### Key Innovation
- Leverages existing LCD manufacturing infrastructure (originally for smartphones, tablets, PCs) to achieve mass production at lower cost than injection molding

### Target Applications
- In-vitro diagnostics
- Liquid biopsy

### MOQ / Lead Times / Pricing
- Oriented toward high-volume production
- Contact required for specifics

## 14. Beonchip (Zaragoza, Spain)

**Website:** https://beonchip.com/

**Founded:** 2016 (spin-off from University of Zaragoza)

**Specialty:** Organ-on-chip platforms; reducing animal testing in drug development.

### Products
- **BE-Flow:** 2D/3D culture under flow; two independent channels; compatible with any pump system or rocker
- **BE-Gradient:** Electrochemical gradient application to 3D cultures; central chamber + two lateral channels connected via 5 microchannels
- **BE-Transflow:** Culture well + microfluidic channel joined by porous membrane
- **BE-Doubleflow:** Two perfusable channels connected via porous membrane (most advanced)

### Materials
- Exclusively COP (Cyclo Olefin Polymer) and COC (Cyclic Olefin Copolymer) medical-grade plastics

### MOQ / Lead Times / Pricing
- Products available for purchase (catalog-based)
- Contact required for pricing

## Selection Guidelines

### For Prototyping (1-50 units)
- **PDMS:** uFluidix, BlackHole Lab (DIY station)
- **Thermoplastic:** Parallel Fluidics (3-day turnaround), Microfluidic ChipShop (micromilling)
- **Glass:** Micronit, IMT AG

### For Small Batch (50-1,000 units)
- **Thermoplastic:** uFluidix, Parallel Fluidics, Microfluidic ChipShop
- **Glass:** Micronit, IMT AG, Toppan

### For Volume Production (1,000-1M+ units)
- **Thermoplastic injection molding:** Vantiva, STRATEC, Microfluidic ChipShop, WenHao
- **Glass:** Toppan, Micronit
- **Silicon MEMS:** MEMSCAP, X-FAB

### For Specialized Applications
- **Organ-on-chip:** Beonchip, Micronit
- **Droplet generation at scale:** Dolomite (Telos system)
- **IVD/diagnostics consumables:** STRATEC, Vantiva
- **In-situ TEM:** Hummingbird Scientific
- **Genomics/proteomics IFCs:** Standard BioTools
- **Passive mixing:** Hummingbird Nano
